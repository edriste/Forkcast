# Install on your phone

Forkcast can be installed as a PWA: it gets a home-screen icon and opens in its own window. No app store is needed.

## Android

1. Open your Forkcast address in Chrome.
2. Choose **⋮ → Add to home screen → Install** (or **Install app**).
3. Open Forkcast from its new icon.

## iPhone and iPad

1. Open your Forkcast address in Safari.
2. Choose **Share → Add to Home Screen**.
3. Enable **Open as Web App**, if shown, and tap **Add**.

Forkcast needs an internet connection. When an update is available, use **Reload app** after saving unfinished work. On iPhone/iPad, enable device notifications from the installed Home Screen app.

See [Apple's instructions](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/open-as-web-app-iphea86e5236/ios) and [Chrome's instructions](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/9658361?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid&hl=en).
