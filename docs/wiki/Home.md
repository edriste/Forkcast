# Forkcast

Plan lunch with your colleagues: share restaurant recommendations, vote on where to eat, organize office cooking and coordinate group orders.

## Use the app

- [Using Forkcast](https://github.com/edriste/Forkcast/wiki/Using-Forkcast)
- [Install on your phone](https://github.com/edriste/Forkcast/wiki/Install-on-your-phone)

## Run your own instance

Choose one deployment:

- [Cloudflare + Supabase Cloud](https://github.com/edriste/Forkcast/wiki/Cloudflare-and-Supabase): a hosted PWA with a managed backend.
- [Self-hosting](https://github.com/edriste/Forkcast/wiki/Self-hosting): run the web app and Supabase on your own server.

Then configure [sign-in](https://github.com/edriste/Forkcast/wiki/Authentication), [maps and location providers](https://github.com/edriste/Forkcast/wiki/Maps-and-location), and optional [device notifications](https://github.com/edriste/Forkcast/wiki/Device-notifications).
