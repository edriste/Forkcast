# Using Forkcast

Sign in, then create an organization or join with an invitation. Choose an office location when creating a team. The organization name in the header lets you switch between teams.

## Plan today's lunch

On **Overview**, choose going out, eating in the office, ordering in, or no lunch/away. The team totals and today's plan update automatically. Lunch days and event deadlines use **Europe/Berlin**.

Nominate a restaurant, vote on today's shortlist, or use **Spin the wheel**. The wheel uses eligible nominations first; without a shortlist, it chooses from reviewed places matching the current Places filters. Favorites alone do not enter the wheel.

Joining office cooking selects eating in the office for that day; joining an order selects ordering in. Leaving clears an automatically selected answer while preserving a choice you made manually.

## Find and save restaurants

**Places** combines map and list views, with cuisine, rating and distance filters. The office pin appears even before anyone has reviewed a restaurant.

Search nearby spots, leave a star rating and optional comment, or bookmark a favorite. Each person's latest review counts once per restaurant and organization; you can edit it later. Favorites are private until you nominate a place for the team.

Cuisine corrections apply only to your organization. **Edit on OpenStreetMap** opens the public editor, where you need your own OSM account. Public edits can take time to reach the app's provider.

## Cook together

Create a meal with a date, portions, price, optional dietary tags and signup deadline. Colleagues can join, change portions and ask questions in the comments. After signup closes, existing attendees can still reduce portions or leave.

Organizers see attendees and payment status and can confirm payments. PayPal links open externally; Forkcast does not verify bank transfers automatically. Unresolved payment alerts stay visible until settled.

## Order food together

Start an order from **Overview → Ordering in**. Choose who will place it, add a menu link and deadline, and let colleagues enter their items and mark themselves ready.

The organizer closes edits, copies the combined order, purchases it externally and records delivery fees and arrival. Forkcast splits the delivery fee and tracks each person's payment; it does not submit orders to delivery services.

## Manage your team

Under **Your team**, administrators manage the office, invitations, members and roles. Multiple administrators are supported, and at least one must remain. Removing someone rotates the invitation code. Leaving or removing a member preserves their reviews, bookings and payment history.

The header bell opens the in-app inbox. [Device notifications](https://github.com/edriste/Forkcast/wiki/Device-notifications) are optional. Language (English/German) and light/dark appearance follow system settings.
